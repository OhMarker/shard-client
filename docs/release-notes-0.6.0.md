Every Shard player now sees the capes other Shard players equipped, and you earn Shard tokens while you play: 10 tokens for every 10 minutes (standing idle for five minutes pauses it). Spend them on capes in Shard Launcher 0.3.0. A pop-up tells you when you earn tokens; the new Tokens HUD element shows your balance. Sign-in uses Mojang's own server check, so your account details never reach Shard.

Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric Loader 0.19+, Fabric API. Use with Shard Launcher 0.3.0+. Capes show on online-mode servers.
