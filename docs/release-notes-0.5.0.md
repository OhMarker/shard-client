The cape you equip in Shard Launcher now shows in-game on your own player, in third person and on your elytra (switchable). High-resolution capes such as the 4K OhMarker cape are drawn smooth and sharp, with mipmaps so they do not flicker. Only you see it; nothing is sent to the server.

Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric Loader 0.19+, Fabric API. Works with Shard Launcher 0.2.0+ (the launcher hands over the equipped cape).
